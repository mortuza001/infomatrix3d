Host Your Own Tronbyt Server

Quick and simple instructions to deploy a Tronbyt server on Railway.

Step 1: Deploy Using the Railway Template

Railway provides a pre-built template that handles the setup automatically.

  • Click the deployment button below to open Railway.
  • Log in using your GitHub account and click deploy.
Deploy Tronbyt on Railway

Step 2: Access Your Server & Setup

Tip: Railway automatically assigns a public URL to your service once deployment finishes. Click on your deployed service to find your live link.
  • Open your generated Railway service URL in your browser.
  • Create your administrator account when prompted on first launch.
  • Navigate to the Firmware menu option to configure your device and connect your hardware.
  • Click "add New Tronbyt", input your name.
  • Choose device type "Tronbyt S3 Wide" or "Tronbyt S3", and ensure "Require API Key" is checked, then click save.
  • Select the device and edit it, then find the image URL, copy the URL, and paste it into your Infomatrix3d dashboard "App URL" field.
