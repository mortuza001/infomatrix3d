More than a scrolling ticker

Stock, Crypto, Sports and Productivity Features

Customize the display for the information and visual modes that matter most to you.

Stocks, Crypto, Forex and Commodities

Track configurable symbols such as stocks, market indexes, cryptocurrencies, foreign exchange pairs and supported commodity futures. Choose text grids, line charts or candlestick charts across time ranges from one hour to one year.

Price Alerts and Custom Names

Create upper or lower price alerts for selected symbols. When a condition is met, the price can flash and the device can sound an audible alert. You can also show supported logos and assign easy-to-read ticker names.

Live Scores Across Major Sports

Follow selected teams or all available games across the NBA, NFL, NHL and MLB, plus NCAA basketball, football and baseball, international cricket, the FIFA World Cup, English Premier League and UEFA Champions League.

Time, Weather and Night Mode

Display local time and weather in Celsius or Fahrenheit, use automatic timezone settings and schedule night mode to reduce brightness during selected hours.

Multi-Mode Commute Tracking

View estimated travel information for private vehicles, buses, rail transit or train and park-and-ride trips. Configure an origin, destination, station and active commute window.

Custom Messages and RSS Feeds

Configure up to three colour-coded text banners or RSS feed URLs for announcements, headlines, promotions and personalized information.

GIF Animation Upload

Upload animated GIFs and resize them for your displays using stretch, fit or crop modes with an on-screen preview.

Custom Apps and App Catalog

Advanced users can display compatible WebP-based custom apps, including Tronbyt and Tidbyt Pixlet content, and select content from the integrated app catalog.

MQTT Message Integration

Connect the ticker to an MQTT broker and display JSON or plain-text messages from a configured topic, making it useful for smart-home events, system notifications and custom automations.

Audio Visualizer

Turn InfoMatrix into a real-time music visualizer with high-speed DDP pixel streaming and compatible tools such as LedFx.

Configurable Pomodoro Timer

Set custom work and break durations, then activate Focus Mode from the dashboard or by holding the physical device button.

Complete Browser Dashboard

Adjust brightness, content durations, sports teams, market symbols, data sources and advanced integrations from a phone or computer. Preview the LED display live, export or import settings and restore factory defaults when needed.

Frequently asked questions

InfoMatrix Stock and Sports Ticker FAQ

What market data can InfoMatrix 3D display?

InfoMatrix can display configurable stocks, market indexes, cryptocurrencies, forex pairs and supported commodity futures using text grids, line charts or candlestick charts.

Which sports are supported?

The dashboard supports the NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, NCAA basketball, NCAA football, NCAA baseball, cricket, the FIFA World Cup, English Premier League and UEFA Champions League.

Can I display my own content?

Yes. You can configure text banners and RSS feeds, upload animated GIFs, receive MQTT messages and use compatible WebP-based custom apps.

Can I control what appears and for how long?

Yes. The browser dashboard provides individual display durations for stocks, weather, messages, GIFs, commute information and supported sports categories.

Does InfoMatrix 3D require a subscription?

No. InfoMatrix 3D does not require a monthly device subscription and includes lifetime firmware updates.

Does it require a phone app or daily computer connection?

No app is required. After setup, the Wi-Fi ticker runs independently and can be managed through its browser-based dashboard from a phone or computer.
